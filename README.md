# GuildBank-App
GuildBank-App

This app only works for TBC anniversary.


Hello chat,

You get to play by going into releases which is in the right side panel of the screen.
Download the latest version for your OS, (only for windows)

Download, double click, run.

Once open you gm and input your wallet address,
and your 2 pgp keys, exactly copy pasted from the passport.

if you lost your pgp keys you can go back to guildbank.biz, go to user and generate and update your new pgp password.

Choose youre game vanilla-plus you need to add the .exe directory and your account number
after that you are good to go click play and complete quests

There are 2 quests daily and weekly.
daily quests consist of winning a daily battleground and having it logged
weekly quests consist of beating a weekly raid and having it logged
