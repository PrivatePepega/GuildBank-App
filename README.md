# GuildBank-App
GuildBank-App

Hello chat,

You get to play by going into releases which is in the right side panel of the screen.
Download the latest version for your OS.
This app only works for TBC anniversary.

Download, double click, run.
Once open you gm and input your wallet address,
and your 2 pgp keys, exactly copy pasted from the passport.
The password = your key 1 + key 2 and sha-256 hashed.
(if you lost your pgp keys you can go back to guildbank.biz, go to user and generate and update your new pgp password.

Thats about it, you can open your game and input the required data,
example: world of warcraft you need account#  and game directory.
